# Personal knowledge taxonomy and model proposals

## Categories, instances, and evidence

The owner knowledge taxonomy classifies **what a reviewed item says**. A journal entry may be a thought, idea, genuine question, decision, goal, learning, or meaningful unclassified reflection. A classification outcome may identify a conversation topic, explicit skill evidence, an explicit profile update, or no assertion. A profile update may concern job title, home location, credential, or employment. These names are sourced from the [processor](../../raw/v1/knowledge-processor.mjs). `None`, low confidence, routine commands, and unsupported inference are recorded decisions but never published owner facts. A topic label supports retrieval; it does not prove expertise. A question does not prove a skill. The owner may correct a category during review.

A taxonomy **category** is a stable kind with a definition and parent; an owner **item** is a source-backed instance. The category hierarchy is: knowledge item → journal entry → the seven journal kinds; knowledge item → candidate assertion → conversation topic, skill evidence, profile update → the four profile fields. `No assertion` is an outcome, not an instance type. Topic labels may form a separate, owner-reviewed subject hierarchy; a model may propose a broader or related subject, but it cannot publish the relation. Category definitions and hierarchy are shared vocabulary. Private item labels, source excerpts, and review actions remain owner-scoped.

Each instance retains owner, source turn, exact excerpt, category identity and version, result/task identity, model and prompt version when generated, review identity, and current interpretation. A correction retains the original text and category. A relation between two private items requires two owner-visible endpoints and an explicit reviewed decision. A retired category maps to a versioned successor without rewriting the original classification. The [source shapes](../../raw/v1/knowledge-shapes.ttl) establish evidence and review fields; the hierarchy above is a v2 [slice decision](../plan/10-knowledge-taxonomy/decisions.md), not a claim about the old store.

## Model proposals and publishing

A model may suggest labels, categories, definitions, and relations for a bounded owner source. Every proposed personal claim or relation carries exact source text, owner/source identity, and model/prompt version. An exact excerpt match is a validity check, not proof that the interpretation is correct. A suggestion remains private and pending until the owner accepts or corrects it. Rejection and later revocation remove it from current owner reads while preserving review history. Duplicate result identity with identical content has one effect; a changed result under that identity is a conflict. A model timeout, empty result, or invalid evidence has a visible outcome and publishes nothing.

The [pinned concept-graph guide](../../raw/orc/ONTOLOGY.md) describes general graph memories and a bundled failure/success/problem taxonomy for its own self-improvement loop. That bundled taxonomy has no role as AWP's personal categories. Its model-backed concept discovery and deterministic graph import are candidate paths; neither grants authority to turn a model suggestion into accepted personal knowledge. Graph search, traversal, and export are owner-scoped, reflect current review decisions, and can be rebuilt from the authoritative history. A graph-only edge or stale concept cannot become an accepted fact through a read path.

## Qualification

The model path needs a reviewed, privacy-safe corpus with positive, negative, ambiguous, correction, and cross-owner cases. Record false published additions, missed durable items, exact excerpt validity, semantic support for labels and category assignments, wrong broader/related edges, and owner-review agreement. Evaluate a pinned model, prompt, taxonomy version, and corpus digest. Thresholds are chosen and recorded before looking at the result. Structural checks and a handful of synthetic examples establish the protocol only; they cannot qualify model quality. Existing private migration reconciliation and public deployment remain independent gates. See [slice 10](../plan/10-knowledge-taxonomy/plan.md).
