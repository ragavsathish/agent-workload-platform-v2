Present each frozen unlinked metadata record and its quarantine reason. The owner can leave it unresolved, record a verified source link, or approve exclusion as non-knowledge metadata with a reason and evidence. Check source links against frozen evidence; a label alone is insufficient. Keep every resource in accounting and rollback material. Export a private disposition package from replayable decisions for the shadow qualifier, with the frozen snapshot identity and unresolved records intact.
