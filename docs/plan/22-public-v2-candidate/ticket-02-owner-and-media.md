Cites [slice plan](plan.md), [candidate rules](../../wiki/public-v2-candidate.md), [scenarios](spec.feature), and [public deployment rules](../../wiki/public-deployment.md).

the owner can read and signal from a distinct authorized device. Confirm a

headers, local hints, or inner credentials. Send timed audio and record a
reachable media path, non-silent reply, settled turn, and private authority
comparison. Record a failed media path separately. Acceptance:
`candidate-004` through `candidate-007`.
